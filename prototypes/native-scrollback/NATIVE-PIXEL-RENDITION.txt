Oracle and antialiasing:
  Reuses per-UTF-16 AX bounds plus clipping viewport containment AND
  AXVisibleCharacterRange agreement. No offscreen or partially clipped sample
  passes. AX native layout is checked independently of emitted rendition bytes.
  CGWindow selection requires Screen Recording access, one running active
  Terminal PID, one on-screen layer-zero marker window, and exact live title.
  Zero/ambiguous matches refuse capture; title is used in memory only.
  Bounds/title/CG ID are rechecked after capture to reject movement/replacement.
  Only the matched CGWindowID is captured; never full-screen or other windows.

  The typed AX helper selects the known synthetic range to verify its text,
  then clears selection and verifies zero length AND empty selected text before
  sampling. Terminal collapses selection at its live cursor, not the requested
  scrollback index; checking the latter was an incorrect oracle constraint.
  Points map to fully contained pixels by measured uniform scale and inward
  rounding. Invalid dimensions, nonuniform scale and malformed data refuse.

  Background distance >24 defines candidate ink; distance >=96 defines strong
  ink. Require max(3, ceil(box area*0.01)) strong pixels and >=90% colour match.
  Default text is accepted on the independently known foreground/background
  colour segment, with residual <=16, rather than near-black pixel equality.
  The first text attempt's strong median was (33,33,33); only 62/106 pixels
  were within an overly strict 48 of black. A failing grayscale-antialias test
  preceded the colour-segment correction; a tinted negative control fails.
  Blue keeps its >=40 channel margin; antialiasing is not treated as exact RGB.

  Controls justify separation, not universal calibration: blank maximum
  background distance is 1; all ASCII strong-ink matches are 1.0; the minimum
  ASCII strong count is 24, well above the minimum 5 in a 14x30 box. The blue
  median is (103.5,92,224), with all 152 strong pixels satisfying the independent
  chromatic condition. Thresholds are prototype choices, not product budgets.
  Black/white only is enforced. Theme/font/general-gamma portability is untested.
